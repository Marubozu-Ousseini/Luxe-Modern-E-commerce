# Marketing & Customer Psychology Optimizations

Principles
- Reduce choice friction: surface one primary action per view.
- Use scarcity and social proof conservatively: limited stock + verified quotes.
- Increase perceived value: show savings vs original price and free-shipping thresholds.
- Loss aversion: highlight what they lose ("Quantités limitées") and guarantee (returns).

Tactics & Examples
- Hero CTA: primary line + microcopy: "Livraison offerte dès 50 000 XAF · Retours 14 jours".
- PDP microcopy near CTA: short value line (20–30 chars) + trust tag: "Paiement sécurisé · Retours gratuits".
- Cross-sell phrasing: "Complétez le look" not "Vous aimerez aussi".
- Promo messaging: emphasize time or quantity remaining when applicable.

Examples (copy)
- Hero: "Matières naturelles. Coupe pensée pour durer." CTA: "Découvrir la boutique".
- PDP CTA: "Ajouter au panier — Paiement sécurisé" (sticky bar label shows price+CTA).
- Banner variants: Free shipping threshold, Time-limited discount, Limited-stock alert.

Measurement
- A/B test CTA phrasing, urgency presence, and promo variants.
